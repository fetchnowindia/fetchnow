Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to fulfil your order: name, mobile number, addresses and item details. It is used solely to complete your delivery and is never sold. We do not store card or UPI details; payments are handled securely by your UPI app. To have your information removed, contact support.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • If you cancel before a rider is assigned, you get a full refund.
  • If you cancel after the rider has left for pickup, a ₹20 charge may apply.
  • If we fail to deliver or lose your item, you get a full refund.
  • Online payment refunds reach the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
  • Cash on Delivery orders are not refunded; we will correct the error instead.
  • For any refund, contact support with your order ID.
